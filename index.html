Frequently asked questions

How do I take attendance for a class or team on my iPhone?
Add your roster once. Each session, tap Take attendance: everyone starts Present and you tap only the exceptions. It takes seconds and works offline.

Can I export an attendance report?
Yes. The PDF has per-person totals and percentages plus the full register grid by date, with a signature line. CSV export is there for spreadsheets and portals.

Does it work for sports teams, clubs, and church groups?
Yes. A group is anything with a roster.

Is my roster private?
Everything stays on your iPhone. No account, no cloud, no tracking.
