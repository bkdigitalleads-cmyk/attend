Attendance Tracker: Check In — Support

Attendance Tracker: Check In is a roll-call app for iPhone. Add a roster, take attendance in one tap per session, and export an attendance report as PDF or CSV.

How do I take attendance?

Open a group and tap Take attendance today. Everyone starts Present. Tap A, L, or E next to anyone who is absent, late, or excused. Each tap saves immediately. Use the arrows at the top of the roll-call screen to move the session to an earlier date.

How do I add people?

In a group, switch to People. Type a name and press return, or tap Paste a whole roster and paste one name per line. Tap a person to rename them, add a note, mark them inactive (keeps their history), or delete them.

How do I get the report?

Report tab → pick the group → Create PDF. The PDF has a summary per person (present, late, absent, excused, percentage) plus the full register grid by date, with a signature line. Export CSV gives one row per person per session. Exports are part of Pro.

How do I restore a purchase?

Settings → Restore purchases. Make sure you are signed into the same Apple Account you used to buy Pro.

What does Pro cost?

Weekly $4.99 after a 3-day free trial, Yearly $29.99 after a 7-day free trial, or Lifetime $59.99 once. Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled in your Apple ID settings. Prices in USD; local pricing may vary.

Is my data private?

Yes. Rosters and marks live only on your phone. No account, no cloud, no tracking. See the privacy policy.
